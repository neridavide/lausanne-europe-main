Foundations

Core documents of the Lausanne Movement

The statements that have shaped evangelical mission for fifty years, each the fruit of a global Lausanne Congress.

  • 1974 The Lausanne Covenant ↗ Core beliefs and commitments for global mission: evangelism, discipleship and social responsibility.
  • 1989 The Manila Manifesto ↗ From the Second Congress: holistic mission, the transformation of society and the proclamation of the gospel.
  • 2010 The Cape Town Commitment ↗ From the Third Congress: today’s mission challenges and the centrality of Christ and his gospel.
  • 2024 The Seoul Statement ↗ From the Fourth Congress, marking fifty years since Lausanne 1974.
