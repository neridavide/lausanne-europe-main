YLG Europe 2026 Gathering

Younger leaders from across Europe gathering to meet, receive teaching, share testimonies, encourage one another, and strategise.

Gathering

Our gatherings are organised so that younger leaders across the continent can meet, receive teaching, share testimonies, encourage each other, and strategise. We are committed to providing a forum for younger leaders to find the tools and support to continually grow in their leadership and to finish the race well.

To discover Lausanne’s vision for younger leaders, find out more about YLGen Europe ↗.

Register

Please register, or send this invitation to a younger leader you know. We are especially looking for people in their mid to late 20s, while considering younger leaders aged 20–40. If you know someone who could benefit from or contribute to this community, we would love to extend an invitation to them.

To join as a mentor, contact ylgen@lausanneeurope.org.

Location

This year we are going to beautiful Durrës, Albania. We will be staying at the Royal G Max Hotel & Spa ↗, beautifully situated beside the beach.

The hotel has offered special rates for anyone arriving early or staying longer: €59 for a double room and €95 for a single room. Bring your spouse along if you wish. Please do not book your hotel room separately; the gathering team will book it for you.

Speakers & contributors

Speaker

Saša Nikolinović

Sarajevo Evangelical Church · Evangelical Alliance

Pastor, artist, and bridge-builder from Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina. Saša has led Sarajevo Evangelical Church since 2001 and serves as Secretary of the Evangelical Alliance in Bosnia. He is committed to helping people encounter Christ in a society shaped by religious, cultural, and historical complexity.

Alongside his pastoral ministry, Saša is a visual artist, bringing together faith, creativity, and social engagement through exhibitions, workshops, and public conversations. He will help us reflect on how God forms us in uncertain times and calls us into His work across Europe.

Speaker

Evi Rodemann

Lausanne Movement · YLGen

Evi is from Hamburg, Germany, and is passionate about youth, younger leaders, Europe, and gatherings that create lasting impact. She serves with the Lausanne Movement and YLGen, chairs the global YLGen commitment for Groups & Gatherings, and produces the Younger Leaders Gathering in Brazil in 2027.

She has a PhD in theology focused on event impact, bringing together Lausanne experience, European vision, and a deep commitment to the next generation of leaders.

Speaker

Jeff Fountain

Schuman Centre for European Studies · Director

Jeff lives in Amsterdam, where he and his wife Romkje lead the Schuman Centre for European Studies, equipping disciples to engage in the public square. He has a background in journalism and history and served as YWAM Europe director from 1990 to 2009.

Jeff has travelled extensively in Europe, chaired the Hope for Europe Round Table for many years, and has written the Weekly Word blog since 2001. His books include Living as People of Hope and Deeply Rooted, the Robert Schuman story.

Contributor

Usha Reifsnider

Lausanne Europe · Regional Director

Usha is a British South Asian Christ-follower from a Hindu background. She and her husband Matt have served migrants, refugees, and diaspora communities for more than three decades. Her research interests sit at the intersection of cultural anthropology and practical theology.

Her ministry includes serving with the Centre for Missionaries from the Majority World, training churches and mission agencies in diaspora ministry, and teaching philosophical frameworks and ethics at the postgraduate level.

Contributor

Jim Memory

Lausanne Europe · Regional Director

Jim is a member of ECM’s International Leadership Team. He and his wife Christine planted churches in Spain from 1994 to 2008 before he moved into mission leadership. He teaches postgraduate modules on European Mission through All Nations Christian College.

Jim is a founding editor of Vista, a research-based journal on issues for mission in Europe, and the author of Europe 2021, a report on the context for mission in Europe today.

Mentor / workshop

Tim Yearsley

LICC · Creative Disruptor, Innovator & Mentor

Tim serves as LICC’s Creative Disruptor, helping develop fresh ways to equip Christian young adults to live out their faith in everyday culture, especially during the first decade of work. He brings creativity, humour, and a love for thoughtful experimentation to his work and leadership.

Tim will mentor participants and lead a workshop on innovative leadership in complex times.

Mentoring

Mentorship and practical help from senior leaders are central to the gathering. Experienced leaders will walk alongside younger leaders, sharing wisdom and experience to help them continue as influencers and agents of transformation in their communities and nations.

Mentors include: Ole-Magnus & Kari Olafsrud, Tim Yearsley, and Jetteke Noordzij.
