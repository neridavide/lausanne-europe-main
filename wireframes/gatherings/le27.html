Why LE27

In November 2021, Lausanne Europe held our first European conference for a generation. The COVID-19 pandemic caused us to postpone, and ultimately cancel, our original plans for an in-person gathering in Poland. Yet God was faithful, and over 1,200 leaders joined us online for LE2021: Dynamic Gospel New Europe ↗.

For the last five years, under the leadership of Co-Regional Directors Jim Memory and Usha Reifsnider, Lausanne Europe has followed a strategy of developing national Lausanne movements. National Movement Gatherings in Budapest (2023) and Cullera (2025) have helped leaders from different countries connect, learn from one another, and bless the church in many of the nations of Europe.

In March 2027, some 130 younger leaders from Europe will travel to São Paulo for YLG4 ↗, joining a ten-year journey of discipleship, mentoring, and collaboration. LE27 brings the National Movement Gathering and the YLG Europe Gathering together, so these younger leaders can become active participants in their national movements, YLG Europe, and the wider Lausanne Europe network.

  • National Movement leadersFrom each of our forty European countries.

  • Issue Network CatalystsLausanne Issue Network Catalysts based in Europe.
