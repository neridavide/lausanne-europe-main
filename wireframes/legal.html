Privacy policy

Lausanne Europe is part of the Lausanne Movement. This policy explains how we use personal information when you visit this website or engage with us. The Lausanne Movement’s Privacy Statement gives further information about its practices worldwide.

Who is responsible for your information

The data controller for this website is [Legal entity name, registered address]. For privacy enquiries, contact privacy@lausanne.org.

Information we collect

We collect the information you give us when you:

  • send an enquiry through the contact form: your name, email address, country, enquiry type and message;
  • subscribe to our newsletter: your first and last name, email address and a record of your consent, such as when you gave it;
  • register for a gathering or event: the details requested on the registration form;
  • make a donation: your name, contact details and gift details. Payments are handled by [Payment provider]; we do not store your full card details;
  • use the coordinator area as a communications coordinator: your account details and the profile information you submit, including through our JotForm forms. Your name, country and role are visible to other signed-in coordinators.

When you use the site, we and our service providers also receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit.

How and why we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, send the updates you ask for, run events, process donations, support the network of communications coordinators, and keep the website secure and working well. Depending on the activity, we rely on your consent (for example, newsletters), on what is needed to carry out an agreement with you (for example, an event registration or donation), on legal obligations (for example, keeping donation records), or on our legitimate interest in running and improving our ministry and website.

Sharing and international transfers

We share information with Lausanne staff and with service providers that help us run the website and our activities, such as Mailchimp, which sends our newsletter, and [Hosting, form and payment providers]. We do not sell your personal information. Lausanne is a global movement, so some recipients are outside the European Economic Area or the UK. Where we transfer information internationally, we use appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, and for any longer period required by law. For example, newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe, and donation records are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to receive a copy of it, and to object to or restrict its use. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. You can unsubscribe from newsletter emails using the link in any email. To make a request, email privacy@lausanne.org. You also have the right to complain to the data protection supervisory authority where you live or work.

Cookie policy

Cookies are small files that websites can store on your device. At present, this website does not set or read cookies, and it does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

We use browser local storage only to remember when you choose to dismiss a campaign notice. This information stays in your browser, is not sent to us, and is not a cookie. You can remove it at any time by clearing this website’s stored data in your browser settings.

Some links take you to services run by other organisations, including Stripe, Mailchimp and JotForm. Those services have their own privacy and cookie practices when you visit them.

If we introduce non-essential cookies or similar technologies in future, we will update this policy and ask for any consent required before using them.

External links

We link to websites run by others. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies when you visit.

Availability and accuracy

We aim to keep information accurate and the website available, but content can change and access may sometimes be interrupted.
