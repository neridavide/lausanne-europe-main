National Lausanne movements for every European country

A national Lausanne movement enables leaders to connect with Lausanne in their nation and in their national language. The information, influence, and resources of Lausanne’s global network can be shared more easily with the national church. And the voice of the church in that country, its theological and missiological reflection and their stories of what God is doing, can be shared more easily with the global church.
